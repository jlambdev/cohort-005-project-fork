# Course Platform

A platform where learners buy courses and watch their lessons. Staff handle support matters such as refunds.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask, with a reason, to get their money back for a purchased course. It stays pending until staff approve or decline it, and the learner keeps access to the course meanwhile.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner's card once staff approve a refund request. Applying a refund ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a learner may currently submit a refund request for a purchase. It depends on the refund window, how much of the course they have completed or watched, whether a request is already pending, and whether the seat is a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Refund offer, cancellation offer

**Refund window**:
The 14 days after purchase during which a learner may submit a refund request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Declined refund request**:
A refund request staff have rejected. No money is returned and the learner keeps access to the course. They may submit another refund request while the refund window is open.
_Avoid_: Rejected refund, denied refund
